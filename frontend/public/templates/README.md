# Product Templates

Place your exported Canva template images here.

## Required files

- `stand_template.png` — ReviewTap Stand template (exported from Canva at 300 DPI)
- `card_template.png`  — ReviewTap Card template (exported from Canva at 300 DPI)

## Export instructions

1. Open your Canva design
2. File → Download → PNG → 300 DPI
3. Rename and place in this folder
4. The app loads them as the locked canvas background layer

## Placeholder

The app ships with SVG placeholder templates for development.
Replace them with your real Canva exports before going live.
